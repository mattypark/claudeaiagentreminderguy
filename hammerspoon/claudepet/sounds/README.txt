Drop your own alert sounds in this folder.

Supported: .mp3  .m4a  .wav  .aiff  .mp4  .aac  .caf
(MP4 works — only the audio track is used.)

Then: pet menu -> Sound effects -> Reload pet, and your file appears in the
picker under its filename. Pick a different one for each event if you want:
session done, waiting on you, session closed.

Keep them short. A one-second chime beats a five-second clip you hear forty
times a day.
